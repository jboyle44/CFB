Where this data comes from tap to expand
Depth Chart
Position, jersey number, and rank order pulled from Ourlads' team depth chart page. Ourlads also flags class year and transfer status per player.
Recruit / TR
Star rating from CollegeFootballData.com's composite recruiting data, shown for both the player's original high school recruiting class ("Recruit") and, for transfers, their separate transfer portal evaluation ("TR") — these are two different ratings from two different points in a player's career, so both show when both exist.
PFF
2025 season overall PFF College grade (color-coded red → yellow → green, low to high), from a PFF Elite data export. Below it, "Ranked Xth of Y [position]" shows where that grade ranks nationally among every graded FBS player at the same position. Transfers are matched to their 2025 grade regardless of which school they played for then. True freshmen show "FRESHMAN" instead of a dash, since they have no prior college snaps to grade.
CFB27
Overall player rating from EA Sports College Football 27, sourced via TeamCrafters' community ratings database (EA's own site doesn't expose this data in a scrapable form).
Color scale
PFF grade and CFB27 rating are colored on a sliding red → yellow → green scale, low to high, so you can scan a formation and spot the strongest/weakest spots at a glance.
Depth chart and recruiting/transfer data refresh Tuesday and Friday mornings. PFF grades require a manual weekly export from PFF Elite since PFF's data isn't publicly scrapable. CFB27 ratings refresh on a separate schedule tied to EA's own roster update cadence.
