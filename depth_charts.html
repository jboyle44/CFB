Where this data comes from tap to expand
Depth Chart
Position, jersey number, and rank order pulled from Ourlads' team depth chart page. Ourlads also flags class year and transfer status per player.
Recruit:
The player's 247Sports Composite score — an industry-aggregated rating (247Sports, ESPN, Rivals, On3) assigned when the player was a high school recruit. National and position rank shown when available. This number doesn't change after high school, even for transfers.
Transfer:
Shown only for players tagged as transfers. This is a separate 247Sports ranking assigned specifically when the player entered the transfer portal — an independent evaluation of their college performance, not their original recruiting rank.
PFF
PFF College grade for the player, from a PFF Elite subscription export. Not yet wired in — shows "—" until that data is added.
Color scale
Composite score and PFF grade are colored on a sliding red → yellow → green scale, low to high, so you can scan a formation and spot the strongest/weakest spots at a glance.
Depth chart and recruiting/transfer data refresh on the same weekly cadence as the rest of the site. PFF grades require a manual weekly export from PFF Elite since PFF's data isn't publicly scrapable.
