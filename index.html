How BRR is calculated tap to expand
BRR = 14.40 + 0.18×SP+ + 0.86×RankedWins + 0.05×SPWeightedWins − 1.34×BadLosses + 0.60×CloseLosses − 2.99×Losses
SP+
Bill Connelly's SP+ overall power rating for the season (offense + defense + special teams in one number).
RankedWins
Wins against teams that finished in that year's final CFP top 25.
SPWeightedWins
Sum of the SP+ rating of every team beaten. Rewards quality of win on a sliding scale rather than a binary top-25 cutoff.
BadLosses
Losses to teams outside the final top 25 — the costliest category per occurrence in the formula.
CloseLosses
Losses decided by 8 points or fewer. Reads as "at least it was competitive" once total losses is already priced in.
Losses
Total regular-season losses, regardless of opponent quality — the single biggest lever in the formula.
Fit on 275 CFP top-25 team-seasons, 2014–2025 (2020 excluded — uneven COVID-season schedules). In-sample R² = 0.814, out-of-sample (leave-one-year-out) R² = 0.800, mean absolute rank error = 2.16 spots. Deliberately excludes conference affiliation, even though a power-conference indicator improves raw fit, so BRR reflects resume merit rather than the committee's demonstrated ~3.7-point bias toward power conferences. Before the CFP committee releases its first ranking of the season, the "Committee" ranking shown here is the AP Poll, used as a proxy — it switches over automatically once official committee rankings begin.
